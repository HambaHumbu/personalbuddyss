Warranty.

12-month limited warranty. No lifetime promises; just a clear one. [CONFIRM: warranty length]

What is covered

  • Manufacturing defects in parts and labour for 12 months from delivery.
  • Dead on arrival: full replacement or refund within 14 days.
  • Failed factory-tested components: display, buttons, sensors, buzzer, Wi-Fi module, power input.

What is not covered

  • Damage from drops, spills, pets, or power supplies other than 5V USB-C.
  • Units opened or modified by anyone other than us.
  • Normal wear: minor scuffs on the PLA body, buzzer volume drift, RTC battery after 2 years.
  • Pre-order and coming-soon features until they ship.

How to make a claim

Email support@personalbuddy.ca with your order number, a description, and a photo or short video. We reply within two business days. Approved claims are repaired or replaced; if we cannot repair it, we refund the tier price.

Extended warranty

A second year of coverage is available as a $25 add-on at configuration time.
