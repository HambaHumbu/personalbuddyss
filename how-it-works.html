Process

How It Works.

From idea to desk in four steps. Your Buddy is made to order, one unit at a time, in Halifax.

Four steps

Idea to desk.

01

Design

Choose your Buddy. Pick a tier for the electronics inside, then a body style and colours for the outside. Eight bodies, eight colours, or fully custom.

02

Personalize

Pick features with honest status tags, write its greeting, name it, choose its mood. Your configuration lives in a shareable link.

03

We Build

We 3D print the body, assemble the electronics, load your configuration, and run the full factory self-test. One unit at a time, in Halifax.

04

Meet Your Buddy

Free local pickup in Halifax by appointment, or tracked shipping anywhere in Canada. It boots up saying your greeting.
