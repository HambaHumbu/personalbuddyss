Shipping and Returns.

Canada-wide tracked shipping, free Halifax pickup, and made-to-order return terms.

Shipping

Shipping.

  • Canada-wide tracked shipping. Rates are calculated before payment; estimates run about $14 to $22 depending on distance.
  • Free local pickup in Halifax, Nova Scotia, by appointment.
  • Buddies are made to order; shipping starts after your unit passes factory testing. Current lead time is 2 to 3 weeks.
  • Every parcel is packed in protective packaging and insured to its order value.

Returns

Returns.

  • Defects and dead on arrival: report within 14 days with a photo and your order number. We repair, replace, or refund, your choice.
  • Made-to-order standard tiers: because each Buddy is built for you, change-of-mind returns are not accepted once printing has begun.
  • Custom tier: final once you approve the design and quote, unless we fail to deliver what was agreed.
  • Pre-order features: if a pre-order feature is cancelled before your unit ships, you may remove it for a refund of its price or cancel the whole order.

How to start a return

How to start a return.

Email support@personalbuddy.ca with your order number and a description. We reply within two business days with next steps and a prepaid label for defect returns.
