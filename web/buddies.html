The Buddy that talks back.

Concept render

A Raspberry Pi 5 runs a small language model on the device, not in the cloud. It answers questions, sets timers and alarms, reads the weather, and plays your music. It is smaller than the cloud assistants and it will tell you so: expect thoughtful answers, not instant ones.

  • Custom wake word ("Hey Buddy", or its own name)
  • Hardware microphone mute switch and visible listening LED
  • On-device processing by default; any cloud use strictly opt-in
  • Local memory you can wipe by voice or by button
  • Music: Bluetooth, AirPlay, local library, internet radio
