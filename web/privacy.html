Privacy Policy.

Last updated: [CONFIRM: date]

What we collect

  • Order details: name, email, shipping address, and your Buddy configuration. Needed to build and deliver your order.
  • Buddy Club signup: email address only, with your express consent.
  • Support messages: whatever you send us, kept to resolve your issue.
  • Analytics: privacy-friendly, cookieless page statistics (Plausible or Umami). No cross-site tracking, no advertising profiles.

What we do not collect

We do not sell personal information. We do not run third-party advertising trackers. Payment card details go directly to our payment processor (Stripe) and never touch our servers.

Your Buddy and your data

Your Buddy processes its configuration on the device. Wi-Fi models fetch time and weather; no personal data leaves the device for these features. The Voice tier runs its language model on the device by default. Any cloud processing is strictly opt-in, and local memory can be wiped by voice command or by button.

Your rights

Under Canada's PIPEDA, you may request access to your personal information, corrections, or deletion. Contact privacy@personalbuddy.ca [CONFIRM: privacy contact]. If we ship to Quebec, Quebec Law 25 applies, including the right to data portability and the requirement to report confidentiality incidents.

Retention

Order records are kept for 7 years for tax purposes. Buddy Club emails are kept until you unsubscribe. Support messages are kept for 2 years.

Security

Encrypted backups, access limited to the people who build your Buddy, no personal data in logs. See our security notes in the site documentation.
