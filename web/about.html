Build Your Buddy

ABOUT

A small workshop with a simple idea.

Personal Buddy started with a 3D printer, a box of electronics, and a question: what if a desk gadget was made for exactly one person?

We are a new workshop in Halifax, Nova Scotia. We design, print, assemble, and program every Buddy ourselves, one unit at a time. No warehouse, no dropshipping, no mystery supply chain. When you order, your Buddy does not exist yet. Then we make it exist, for you.

Concept render

What we actually do.

Every Buddy is built to order at our Halifax bench:

  • Designed here. The shell, the joints, the face engine, the firmware.
  • Printed here. Each body takes about six hours on our printers.
  • Assembled and wired here. Every sensor, LED, and screen placed by hand.
  • Programmed here. Your choices from the builder are flashed before it ships.
  • Tested here. Nothing leaves the bench until it passes our checkout. [CONFIRM: burn-in and QC procedure]
